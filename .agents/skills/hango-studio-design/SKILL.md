# HANGO Studio Design

## Make the pitch the hero

Give the pitch the dominant visual area and make overlays support, not obscure, the football moment. Reveal one insight at a time through progressive disclosure. Avoid enterprise-dashboard grids, dense control chrome, and undifferentiated metrics.

## Preserve epistemic distinctions

- Render actual and hypothetical geometry with unmistakably different line treatments, color, and labels; never rely on color alone.
- Distinguish observations from model estimates in typography, badges, and explanatory copy.
- Keep verification status and evidence reachable without overwhelming casual viewers.
- Never present interpretive prose as a measured fact.

## Use broadcast-quality craft

Use a restrained dark palette, high-contrast pitch markings, studio-ready typography, crisp diagrams, purposeful motion, and safe-title spacing. Prefer a single decisive composition over a collection of dashboard cards. Make screenshots and broadcast crops legible at a glance.

## Review each view

Confirm that the primary football question is obvious, the actual state is readable before alternatives appear, focus and keyboard behavior work, evidence is accessible, text remains legible, and motion clarifies transitions rather than decorating them.
